Discourse System

The system through which language is organised across sentences, turns, texts, genres and extended communicative events.

00 · LEARNING MAP

Where are we going?

Start: understand discourse as organisation beyond the sentence: how language creates continuity, structure, relationships and larger communicative units.

End: trace cohesion, coherence, information flow, discourse relations, genre, turn organisation and text-level patterning as trainable processes.

02 · DECOMPOSITION STAGES

How the Architecture Is Analysed

COMPONENT

A major functional or structural part of the system.

SUB-COMPONENT

A more specific structure inside a component.

MICRO-COMPONENT

A small identifiable element that can be analysed separately.

PROCESS

What happens during interpretation, construction or use.

MECHANISM

The underlying operation that makes an observable pattern possible.

TRAINABLE UNIT

A teachable and measurable unit that can be practised, observed and improved.
