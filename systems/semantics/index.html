Semantic System

The system through which language encodes, combines and interprets meaning across words, phrases, clauses and propositions.

Where are we going?

Start: understand meaning as an organised system rather than a collection of dictionary definitions.

End: trace how meaning is represented, composed, related, disambiguated and interpreted, then locate trainable units for deeper analysis.

02 · DECOMPOSITION STAGES

How the Architecture Is Analysed

COMPONENT

A major functional or structural part of the system.

SUB-COMPONENT

A more specific structure inside a component.

MICRO-COMPONENT

A small identifiable element that can be analysed separately.

PROCESS

What happens during interpretation, construction or use.

MECHANISM

The underlying operation that makes an observable pattern possible.

TRAINABLE UNIT

A teachable and measurable unit that can be practised, observed and improved.
