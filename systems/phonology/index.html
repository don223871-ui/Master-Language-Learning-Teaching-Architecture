Phonological System

The sound system of a language: how speech sounds are organised, contrasted, combined, stressed, timed and delivered.

00 · LEARNING MAP

Where are we going?

Start: understand the phonological system as a complete structure.

End: identify its components, trace how they operate in speech, and locate the smallest trainable units for pronunciation and listening development.

02 · DECOMPOSITION STAGES

How the System Is Analysed

COMPONENT

A major part of the phonological system, such as syllables, stress or intonation.

SUB-COMPONENT

A more specific structure inside a component, such as onset–nucleus–coda within syllable structure.

PROCESS

What happens during perception or production, such as linking or reduction.

MECHANISM

The underlying phonological operation that produces an observable pattern.

MICRO-COMPONENT

The smallest identifiable feature or contrast worth analysing separately.

TRAINABLE UNIT

A teachable and measurable unit for perception, pronunciation or spoken production.
