Grammatical System

The system through which words, phrases and clauses are structured and combined to express relationships, time, stance, modality and meaning.

00 · LEARNING MAP

Where are we going?

Start: understand grammar as a structured system rather than a list of rules or isolated forms.

End: identify the major grammatical components, trace how they interact in real language use, and locate the processes and mechanisms that can become trainable units.

SYSTEM→COMPONENT→SUB-COMPONENT→MICRO-COMPONENT→PROCESS→MECHANISM→TRAINABLE UNIT

02 · DECOMPOSITION STAGES

How the Architecture Is Analysed

COMPONENT

A major grammatical domain within the system.

SUB-COMPONENT

A more specific grammatical structure inside a component.

MICRO-COMPONENT

A small identifiable grammatical pattern, contrast or choice.

PROCESS

What happens when grammatical information is interpreted, selected or formulated.

MECHANISM

The underlying operation that makes a grammatical pattern or choice possible.

TRAINABLE UNIT

A teachable and measurable grammatical unit that can be practised, observed and improved.
