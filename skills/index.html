02 · DECOMPOSITION STAGES

How the Architecture Is Analysed

COMPONENT

A major functional or structural part of the skill or system.

SUB-COMPONENT

A more specific structure inside a component.

MICRO-COMPONENT

A small identifiable element that can be analysed separately.

PROCESS

What happens during language perception, comprehension, formulation or production.

MECHANISM

The underlying operation that makes an observable pattern or performance possible.

TRAINABLE UNIT

A teachable and measurable unit that can be practised, observed and improved.
