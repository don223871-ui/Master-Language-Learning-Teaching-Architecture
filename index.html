02 · Architecture Logic

How We Will Build It

We will not treat these eighteen areas as simple topics. Each one will be recursively decomposed and then converted into a learning and teaching system.

DOMAINThe broad territory of language.
SYSTEMA major functional system within the domain.
SKILLA teachable and learnable capability.
SUB-SKILLA distinct component of a larger skill.
MICRO-SKILLA precise observable ability.
COMPONENT → MICRO-COMPONENTThe finest operational level needed for analysis and training.
